Audio
Choose what each surface does with the sound under Audio reaction in its properties.
Tempo
Tap along to set the tempo. Use Tempo pulse in a surface's Audio reaction, or uTempo, uTempoPulse and uBar in custom shaders.
Depth & light
Set which way each surface faces under Depth in its properties. The light then shades the faces like a real object, and surfaces set to Cast shadow darken whatever sits behind them. Depth effects use this light too.

Del / Alt+click remove a mask or edge point
Points snap to other shapes and stay joined. Drag with Shift to pull one free, Ctrl to skip snapping

D 3D view · drag to orbit, scroll to spread

O output window · H this screen
