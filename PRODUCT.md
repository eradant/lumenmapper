# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Hobbyists making projection displays at home and for events: Halloween and holiday house projections, parties, small shows, often a first or second attempt at projection mapping. They work on a laptop in a dark room or yard, standing near the projector, alternating between precise setup (lining corners up with a real object) and running the show once it's up. Some grow into small live performances with music, scenes and a MIDI controller.

## Product Purpose

Lumen Pin is a browser-based projection mapper. You trace surfaces onto real objects (corner pin, edge points, mesh), fill them with generated effects, video, text or live inputs, mask out what shouldn't be lit, and run the result as a show: scenes, transitions, music cues, tempo, MIDI, blind editing, multiple projectors and an unattended player mode. Success is a hobbyist going from "projector pointed at a wall" to a running, reliable show in one evening, and trusting it enough to leave it running.

## Positioning

Show-ready: reliable live control (scenes, MIDI, tempo, blind edit, player mode) without the cost or intimidation of pro VJ tools.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, pro. The interface behaves like well-made stage equipment: exact, calm under pressure, never showy. The projection is the star; the UI is the instrument that runs it. Confidence comes from clarity and reliability, not decoration. Borrow the feel of Teenage Engineering (hardware-like precision, a strong graphic voice, controls that feel physical and exact) and Ableton Live's performance view (big readable triggers, dense but calm, made for dark stages).

## Anti-references

- Cyberpunk / neon gamer looks: glowing neon, glitch fonts, RGB-gaming styling.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: card grids, stat tiles, purple gradients.
- Toy or kid's-app styling: bubbly rounded buttons, cartoon color.

## Design Principles

- **The projection is the star.** The UI stays quiet and dark so attention, and light, belong to the stage and the output.
- **Show-ready by default.** Anything needed mid-show (scenes, blackout, master, Go, tempo) is big, obvious and reachable by key or MIDI. Nothing a performer needs is buried in a panel.
- **Precision without intimidation.** Pro-grade exactness (pixel nudges, snapping, numeric values) presented so a first-timer isn't scared off: progressive disclosure over density.
- **Behave like equipment.** Controls are exact, consistent and predictable, like hardware. State is always visible: what's live, what's armed, what's waiting for the beat.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and full keyboard control. Designed for dark rooms: low overall luminance, no large bright areas that flood a darkened space or ruin night vision, and state never carried by color alone. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
